# Reviving inverse bifurcation as landscape science

*Synthesis of six audited research notes · 19 October 2026 · closing section separates facts, recommendations, unknowns*

The thesis *Inferring Bifurcations Between Phenotypes* — LaTeX source dated **29 October 2021**, KCL degree awarded **1 September 2022** — already ended by naming this revival's two targets: *designing limit cycles* and *spatially extended systems* ([ch. 6](https://github.com/gszep/thesis/blob/master/chapters/6.tex)). What it demonstrated was narrower but real: gradient-based inference of codimension-1 **equilibrium** bifurcations with no back-propagation through the continuation solver ([NeurIPS 2021](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2021/hash/2c6ae45a3e88aee548c0714fad7f8269-Abstract.html)); what it left undone is an end-to-end differentiable pipeline for periodic orbits and nonlinear PDE patterns. The searched 2021–2026 sources supply several ingredients — snaking, deflated continuation, periodic-orbit continuation, validated numerics, data-driven descriptors — but no integration was verified in them: an evidence gap, not proof of novelty or absence. We recommend three staged, falsifiable experiments — reproduce the static method with its **paper/code discrepancies made explicit**, then phase-fixed **cycle inference with Floquet stability**, then a nonlinear **Swift–Hohenberg branch/pattern atlas** with discretization convergence and symmetry-aware descriptors — inside a reversible native backend plus portable explorer architecture. No language wins on current evidence: Julia carries the least capability risk for continuation-plus-stability-plus-GPU work; browser precision requires separating Wasm CPU binary64 from WGSL's f32-and-f16 shaders (no f64); Julia's agentic-coding advantage is an untested hypothesis; and formal work should start with a narrow Lean certificate milestone, not a certified-PDE ambition.

## The thesis aimed past its own proof of concept

The unifying aim was methodological: import representation and differentiability into biological engineering, making the design–build–test–learn loop bidirectional and, "in principle, completely differentiable", learning qualitative observations with bifurcations before quantitative details ([ch. 1](https://github.com/gszep/thesis/blob/master/chapters/1.tex)). Three instantiations follow: bifurcation diagrams as training data (ch. 4 / NeurIPS 2021), basis-function fields as qualitative state-space specifications (ch. 2), and interactive cytometry embeddings as qualitative navigation via FlowAtlas.jl ([ch. 5](https://github.com/gszep/thesis/blob/master/chapters/5.tex)). Chapter 3 added collaborative spatial modelling — arc-length continuation of the bistability region, spatial simulations, boundary-velocity inference — for the engineered double-exclusive *E. coli* reporter ([ch. 3](https://github.com/gszep/thesis/blob/master/chapters/3.tex)).

The NeurIPS 2021 paper (Szep, Dalchau & Csikász-Nagy) infers parameters θ so that a deflated-continuation prediction set of codimension-1 bifurcations matches qualitative targets, pairing a geometric-mean error term with a measure pushing optimizers toward bifurcating regimes ([arXiv:2106.04243](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.04243v3)). Gradients come from implicit layers: a bifurcation-point velocity solves an augmented (N+1)-dimensional system (Eq 10), and a generalized Leibniz rule moves differentiation inside the arclength integral via normal deformation fields. Demonstrated on saddle-node and pitchfork minimal models, the genetic toggle switch (800 restarts, 98% in two qualitative clusters), and a 21-parameter *E. coli* circuit that surfaced a previously unknown damped-oscillation regime; the cost landscape "allows us to organise models in terms of topological and geometric equivalence". Stated limits: the determinant is the bottleneck, confining the implementation to ODEs (N<100 tractable on a laptop); Hopf appears only as a sketched eigenvalue measure in Supplementary E; and under its no-circulating-currents statistics, "it is not possible to detect Hopf bifurcations from statistics in homeostasis" ([ch. 2](https://github.com/gszep/thesis/blob/master/chapters/2.tex)).

Cross-reading the paper's arXiv HTML (main text and appendices A–E) against all five released source files shows the code is **not identical to the main-text objective**; the audit verified three distinctions: paper Eq 7 normalizes the measure by arclength, while the code's `measure` functions only **sum weighted measures** (unbounded, scale-dependent on branch length and discovery); the code **squares target distances** where Eq 4 does not; and although the released measure is eigenvalue-based, the matching velocity retains the **determinant-based augmented system** ([Objectives.jl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bifurcationkit/BifurcationInference.jl/master/src/Objectives.jl)). Each `train!` iteration recomputes the full deflated continuation ([Utils.jl](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bifurcationkit/BifurcationInference.jl/master/src/Utils.jl)). None of this is demonstrably an equivalent or improved objective — Stage 1 must decide. The package is registered in the Julia General registry but dormant (last push 2024-06-01, three open issues) ([repo metadata](https://api.github.com/repos/bifurcationkit/BifurcationInference.jl)); OpenAlex records **4 citations**, most substantive an eLife 2025 paper fitting bifurcation structure to reduced neuron models ([citing works](https://api.openalex.org/works?filter=cites:W3168928366)); a JuliaCon 2020 precursor talk projected "designing patterns and limit cycles in partial differential equations" ([pretalx](https://pretalx.com/juliacon2020/speaker/ZBMJRN/)).

The thesis's own future work prescribes the revival. §"Designing Limit Cycles" combines global constraints (a closed surface ∂Ω and an angular-momentum field ω(u) from basis functions) with local eigenvalue constraints — "it is possible to design global bifurcations" — with a worked example of a cycle born in a local Hopf and destroyed in a global infinite-period bifurcation. §"Spatially Extended Systems" proposes differentiable pattern design via discretized PDEs, snaking (spots and stripes) and dispersion-relation gradients, flagging the (NK)² gradient scaling as the limiting factor ([ch. 6](https://github.com/gszep/thesis/blob/master/chapters/6.tex)); the viva's closing slides carry the same two headings ([presentation.jl](https://github.com/gszep/thesis/blob/master/viva/presentation.jl)). Three "landscape" strands must not be conflated: the Waddington metaphor (no algorithm), the θ-cost landscape (implemented, fixed-point bifurcations only), and a cross-model embedding atlas (explicitly hypothetical — no metric or invariance specified). Provenance: the thesis was read as **full LaTeX source text** — all six chapters, front and back matter, appendices, and the viva's future-work slides — while the four **embedded publication PDFs were not read** (the NeurIPS paper was read via arXiv HTML; their content enters via prefaces, afterwords and the proceedings page), and **no compiled complete-thesis PDF was built or read**. The 2021 source and 2022 award dates are distinct fields; neither is the repository's single visible commit (2024-07-08) ([Preamble.tex](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gszep/thesis/master/Preamble.tex), [KCL record](https://kclpure.kcl.ac.uk/portal/en/studentTheses/inferring-bifurcations-between-phenotypes)).

## Existing ingredients and unverified integration gaps

The objects a nonlinear landscape must contain — coexisting branches, folds, disconnected components, localized versus extended states — are established beyond linear Turing analysis: homoclinic snaking organizes localized states in "snakes-and-ladders" fashion ([Burke & Knobloch review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17903009/)), with Swift–Hohenberg localized states adding rolls toward a periodic wavetrain ([2007](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0375960106013612)) and snaking recurring in plane Couette flow ([JFM 2016](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/homoclinic-snaking-in-plane-couette-flow-bending-skewing-and-finitesize-effects/6484E2538F10F3DC307271D531D61256)), shell buckling ([Proc. R. Soc. A 2017](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rspa/article/473/2205/20170177/57457/Fully-localized-post-buckling-states-of)) and radial form ([Gomila 2021](https://academic.oup.com/imamat/article/86/5/1094/6352381)). Mature software computes these landscapes directly: pde2path (Matlab FEM, tutorials through Turing–Hopf mixing) ([NMTMA 2014](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/numerical-mathematics-theory-methods-and-applications/article/pde2path-a-matlab-package-for-continuation-and-bifurcation-in-2d-elliptic-systems/11B12FB6196D66ADCBE37A84944D7DC2), [tutorials](https://pde2path.uol.de/tutorials.html)) and the BifurcationKit ecosystem, with homoclinic-orbit, multi-parameter-continuation, FEM and delay components plus a GPU Swift–Hohenberg tutorial ([bifurcationkit.github.io](https://bifurcationkit.github.io)).

Deflated continuation — developed for PDEs, and the paper's diagram engine — finds additional, possibly disconnected branches but carries **no completeness guarantee** ([Farrell et al. 2016](https://arxiv.org/html/1603.00809v1)); recent combinations with reduced-order and adaptive methods, some preprint-level, broaden discovery further. Periodic-orbit continuation is commodity for ODEs: AUTO-07p has computed equilibria, periodic orbits and their bifurcations since the 1980s ([repo](https://github.com/auto-07p/auto-07p)), now automated from Python ([auto-AUTO, JOSS 2025](https://joss.theoj.org/papers/10.21105/joss.08079.pdf)); BifurcationKit reproduces the classic Hopf-to-periodic-orbit workflow ([pp2 tutorial](https://docs.sciml.ai/BifurcationKit/stable/tutorials/ode/tutorialPP2/)). The frontier — periodic and modulated patterns in PDEs — is tutorial-documented, not turnkey.

Global basin topology can be *certified*: Conley–Morse computation over rigorous outer approximations delivers provably correct Morse decompositions for maps and moderate dimensions ([Mischaikow 2016](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/jcd.2016001), [Batko et al. 2020](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10208-020-09444-1), [CHomP](https://chomp.rutgers.edu/Projects/survey/cmdbSurvey.pdf)) — but no PDE-scale application was found in these searches. Validated continuation certifies periodic orbits in polynomial vector fields ([van den Berg et al. 2021](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/jcd.2021004), [Lessard 2016](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0167278916000294)); no source found certifies a full snaking diagram or a PDE localized-state branch. The data-driven tier (detection, no proof) has grown fast: SINDyCP recovers parameterized pattern-forming dynamics and extrapolates bifurcation predictions beyond weakly nonlinear onset ([PRR 2023](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.5.L042017)); active Bayesian search locates bifurcations under expensive evaluation ([Chaos 2025](https://pubs.aip.org/aip/cha/article/35/5/053159/3347820/Active-search-for-bifurcations)); basin entropy flags basin transformations ([2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16996)); persistent homology classifies Turing-pattern parameter dependence ([2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01552-9)); and ML inverse problems recover pattern-forming parameters from noisy experimental patterns ([iScience 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2589004224010447)). The convergent trend replaces dense sweeps with query-efficient, uncertainty-aware exploration of qualitative structure.

No published end-to-end differentiable cycle- or PDE-pattern-inference pipeline was found. Three directions follow from documented capabilities plus gaps: coupling active discovery to deflated continuation and certification; scaling Conley–Morse databases to PDE pattern problems; and descriptor-to-branch grounding — a metric connecting a measured pattern to a specific branch of a computed landscape. Negative search results delimit this corpus, not the literature; absence cannot establish novelty or impossibility, and several sources were verified at abstract level only.

## Languages without a winner: precision boundaries and the browser

For the algorithmic chain this program needs — arclength and deflated continuation, matrix-free Newton–Krylov and eigensolvers, periodic orbits by shooting and collocation, CPU and GPU — BifurcationKit.jl's documented core matches nearly one-to-one, including codim-2 fold/Hopf and period-doubling/Neimark–Sacker continuation (which presuppose Floquet multipliers) and claimed use "for massive computations (millions of unknowns), on the GPU" ([docs v0.4.17, built 2026-10-03](https://docs.sciml.ai/BifurcationKit/dev/), [HAL paper](https://inria.hal.science/hal-02902346/)) — a **capability inventory, not an independent performance validation**. Julia's browser story has levels but no full-stack port: WGLMakie/Bonito document offline export with limited interactivity, and the experimental WebAssemblyCompiler handles a restricted, type-stable subset untested against BifurcationKit ([WGLMakie](https://docs.makie.org/stable/explanations/backends/wglmakie), [WebAssemblyCompiler](https://tshort.github.io/WebAssemblyCompiler.jl/)).

Python reaches the checklist only as an assembly with seams to test: JAX/PyTorch for AD and f64 arrays, where JAX's x64 mode is a global opt-in whose omission silently truncates to f32 ([JAX docs](https://docs.jax.dev/en/latest/101/default_dtypes.html)); Optimistix/lineax for differentiable Newton-type solves ([Optimistix](https://github.com/patrick-kidger/optimistix)); scipy on CPU for sparse and eigen work; AUTO-07p via wrappers for continuation and Floquet. Sparse GPU linear algebra is a reported bottleneck in JAX workflows ([JAX-AMG](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09001v1)). Browser compute is subset-only: Pyodide runs CPython in Wasm without threading or JAX ([Pyodide constraints](https://pyodide.org/en/stable/usage/wasm-constraints.html)) — narrower than impossibility.

wgpu is dual-licensed Apache-2.0/MIT, runs natively on Vulkan/Metal/D3D12/OpenGL and on WebGPU over Wasm (WebGL2 fallback), and has no CUDA backend ([wgpu](https://github.com/gfx-rs/wgpu)); native CUDA from Rust goes through cudarc or NVIDIA's official CUDA Rust track compiling kernels to PTX ([NVIDIA](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/introducing-cuda-rust-two-tracks-for-writing-gpu-kernels/)). The precision boundary is specification-level: **native and Wasm CPU code keep IEEE binary64**, while portable WGSL GPU shaders offer f32 and optional f16 with no f64 ([Wasm types](https://webassembly.github.io/spec/core/syntax/types.html), [WGSL](https://www.w3.org/TR/WGSL/#scalar-types), [open f64 request](https://github.com/gpuweb/gpuweb/issues/2805)); WebGPU's defaults (128 MiB storage bindings, 256 MiB buffers, higher on request) bound GPU resources, not CPU/Wasm state ([WebGPU spec](https://www.w3.org/TR/webgpu/), [wgpu limits](https://docs.rs/wgpu/latest/wgpu/struct.Limits.html)); the WebGL2 fallback must not be assumed to preserve WebGPU compute. Availability is broad as of October 2026 — Chromium desktop since 113 (Linux NVIDIA since 147), Firefox stable on Windows/macOS, Safari 26 — with remaining gaps closing during 2026 ([implementation status](https://github.com/gpuweb/gpuweb/wiki/Implementation-Status)). No NVIDIA measurements were taken: the remote host (`ssh artemis`) was deliberately not accessed.

**Recommendation, not a measured ranking**: a native reference backend — Julia/BifurcationKit the best-documented candidate here — emits versioned branch/spectrum artifacts; a portable explorer (Rust/Wasm CPU f64 plus wgpu visualization) consumes them via a contract-first schema, so the backend can change without touching the shareable UI. Commit only after six gates run locally: f64 continuation correctness against an independent reference (e.g., AUTO); continuation throughput on canonical models; sparse/matrix-free Newton–Krylov behavior; measured f64-versus-f32 kernel throughput on available hardware; explorer transfer size and latency at WebGPU defaults; periodic-orbit/Floquet accuracy on a known-multiplier problem. **No head-to-head benchmark data exists in these notes; nothing was executed.**

## Agentic coding and Lean: mostly open empirical questions

The "two-language problem" originates as a design rationale in Julia's founders' SIAM Review paper, with no agent or defect-rate measurements ([arXiv:1411.1607](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.1607)). The three named repair/editing benchmarks contain no Julia — SWE-bench Multilingual, Multi-SWE-bench and Aider polyglot — a search-limited result, not proof no study exists ([SWE-bench Multilingual](https://www.swebench.com/multilingual.html), [Multi-SWE-bench](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.02605v1), [Aider](https://aider.chat/2024/12/21/polyglot.html)). The widely quoted 43%-versus-63% comparison scores **different datasets** (Multilingual versus Python-only SWE-bench Verified) at one fixed $2.50/task budget, establishing neither a Python advantage nor its cause; Rust scored highest (58%) within the Multilingual run, whose authors flag task-difficulty, harness and single-model limitations. MultiPL-E includes Julia but measures non-agentic code generation ([arXiv:2208.08227](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.08227)). Both pro-Julia arguments (no prototype-to-rewrite step, actionable MethodError feedback, one implementation language) and counterarguments (training-data coverage, harness maturity) are untested. **Recommendation**: an equal-budget, matched-task evaluation — identical scientific tasks in Python, Julia and Rust, one fixed model, disclosed cost/token/turn caps, hidden tests, repeated seeds, separate timings, plus a within-Julia ablation separating stack from dispatch effects; Lean proof work is a separate track, not a comparable coding task.

Lean's documented interop boundary is the C ABI via `lean.h` — explicitly "unstable", no compound structs by value ([FFI reference](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/Run-Time-Code/Foreign-Function-Interface/)). Rust has a high-level wrapper ([lean-ffi](https://github.com/argumentcomputer/lean-ffi)); Python has Pantograph's machine-to-machine proof-search interface and LeanDojo ([arXiv:2410.16429](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16429)); **no maintained Julia–Lean binding was found** (a `ccall` bridge would be bespoke, unverified work). Validation is a version-dependent ladder: kernel acceptance, `#print axioms` (inspection, not a checker), lean4checker's olean replay, and the manual's gold standard `lake comparator` — sandboxed builds with serialized proof-term export replayed by independent checkers (nanoda in Rust; Lean4Lean's complete external typechecker) ([Validating a Lean Proof](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/ValidatingProofs/), [Lean4Lean](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14064v3)). Native computation has introduced dedicated axioms since Lean 4.29, and 4.35.0 (2026-10-12) removed the old reduction machinery — not native evaluation or its additional trust; an external checker accepting a derivation conditional on a native axiom has not verified that computation ([4.35.0 notes](https://lean-lang.org/doc/reference/latest/releases/v4.35.0/)). The integrated checking sandbox is Linux-only — a caveat on this macOS host.

Mathlib's ODE directory provides Picard–Lindelöf existence/uniqueness and Grönwall inequalities — no scheme verification, Floquet theory or bifurcation theory there ([Mathlib ODE tree](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/master/Mathlib/Analysis/ODE)); flows and invariant sets, and tempered distributions with Fourier machinery, live elsewhere in Mathlib ([Flow.lean](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/master/Mathlib/Dynamics/Flow.lean)); LeanCert advertises checked interval certificates at README level, unaudited ([LeanCert](https://github.com/alerad/leancert)). **No end-to-end certified dynamics or PDE pipeline was verified anywhere.** Two distinctions keep claims honest: validated numerics plus a theorem already constitute a computer-assisted proof without Lean — formal checking is an additional trust layer, not the definition of proof; and checking a finite list of interval enclosures is not a theorem about periodic-orbit existence or a continuum PDE — the certificate's semantic implication must itself be proved. **Recommendation**: start with one narrow milestone with explicit semantics — the logistic map's fixed point x* = 1 − 1/r existing and attracting an orbit neighborhood for 1 < r < 3 (the r = 3 flip is next), a discrete-Grönwall contraction bound, or an external rational certificate with its implication proved — sized as a 1–3 agent-session pilot to be measured, not assumed, after inspecting existing libraries.

## Three staged experiments with explicit epistemic labels

**Stage 1 — reproduce static inference, discrepancies explicit.** Pin revisions first; many sources here point at moving `master`/`stable` targets. Reproduce a small equilibrium case (saddle-node, pitchfork, toggle switch) with the released code, then resolve what the audit left open: whether the unnormalized summed measure and squared distances behave identically to paper Eqs 4 and 7–8 or change inference outcomes. Falsification is concrete: gradient checks against finite differences, empty and exact-target edge cases, spectral-multiplicity tests, a direct paper/code normalization experiment; failure or behavioral divergence revises the foundation before any extension.

**Stage 2 — cycle inference with phase condition and Floquet.** Keep three objects separate: damped oscillations at a stable focus (the thesis's *E. coli* by-product), an equilibrium Hopf location, and a fitted stable periodic orbit. First fit Hopf locations with a tracked simple conjugate eigenpair — one real-part-zero condition plus transversality, not two duplicate conjugate conditions — via Hopf-specific augmented systems rather than the determinant. Then infer a periodic orbit: shooting or collocation with unknown period and a phase condition, cycle observables as targets, Floquet multipliers for stability ([BifurcationKit shooting docs](https://docs.sciml.ai/BifurcationKit/stable/periodicOrbitShooting/)). Because no-currents steady-state statistics cannot see cycles, the experiment must state what temporal or perturbational information resolves that ambiguity. Failure to recover known cycles in a synthetic model (e.g., the thesis's basis-function construction, after checking smoothness, tangential flow and superposition), or ill-conditioned Floquet sensitivities, falsifies the extension before PDE work begins.

**Stage 3 — nonlinear Swift–Hohenberg branch/pattern atlas.** Go beyond linear Turing: deflated continuation of the discretized Swift–Hohenberg equation builds a branch atlas (localized states, snaking, spots and stripes) with explicit spatial-discretization convergence checks (the thesis's (NK)² scaling is an author-reported expectation, not a law), symmetry-aware pattern descriptors to summarize pattern morphology, and comparison against pde2path and BifurcationKit's GPU tutorial ([pde2path](https://pde2path.uol.de/tutorials.html), [BifurcationKit](https://bifurcationkit.github.io)). Persistent homology is one candidate — not a complete invariant, not automatically useful; descriptors compare morphology rather than certify branch topology. Every output carries a tier label — numerical discovery, mathematical certification, or formal checking; nothing is called certified unless a specific theorem covers it. Alongside run the two method experiments — equal-budget agent language evaluation, narrow Lean certificate milestone — both proposals, not completed work.

## Conclusion

The revival is continuity, not reinvention. The thesis's closing headings define the work; its implicit-differentiation-over-continuation architecture, qualitative measures and restart-and-cluster exploration are a promising starting point; and the honest baseline, for the original work inspected here, is that nothing beyond equilibrium codimension-1 inference was demonstrated end-to-end, while surrounding ingredients — snaking, commodity periodic-orbit continuation, validated numerics, topological descriptors — are mature. What this synthesis changes is the location of the binding constraint: not language choice, which no current evidence settles, but validated problem formulations — phase conditions and Floquet derivatives for cycles, discretization convergence for PDE patterns, certificate semantics for formal claims.

The staged program converts each aspiration into a falsifiable gate while keeping infrastructure reversible behind a versioned artifact contract. If Stage 1 shows the paper and the code define different optimization problems, that result still sharpens the revival: assembling the inspected ingredients into one differentiable cycle- and pattern-inference pipeline becomes a tentative, testable integration opportunity rather than a presumption of feasibility or priority. Running the equal-budget language evaluation and the Lean pilot alongside converts hypothesis-driven stack debates into measurements — the thesis's own qualitative-before-quantitative philosophy, applied to its revival.
